Passport · ID Autofill

Reads the passport MRZ, you review, then it fills the Mews profile. Local only — nothing leaves this device.

1 Read the document
Upload a passport photo/scan — flat and in focus works best (a photo of a screen will struggle).
Drag a box around the 2–3 MRZ lines at the bottom of the passport, then “Read selection”.
2 Review
Fill all fills the Profile, then auto-opens + fills + saves the Identity-document and Address sections (skipping any that already have a row). The “only” buttons fill a single section’s form that you’ve opened yourself (no auto-save). Amber fields are guesses — confirm before saving.
